This is the instructor page the book refers to in Appendix C. It exists for one purpose: to get teaching material to people who are teaching from the book, without putting worked answers where a student searching for a test name will find them first.

What is public, and needs no request

  • All 197 solutions are printed in the book, in Appendix C. There is no website paywall on them and there never will be.
  • The companion repository — every arithmetic box regenerated by a script, every figure generated by committed code, and one notebook per chapter.
  • Every figure may be reproduced for classroom use without asking. The copyright page says so. Cite the book and use them.
  • Errata, kept current until August 2028.

Worked code for the 57 [C] exercises

The [C] exercises ship in the repository as failing tests, and making them pass is the exercise, so the printed appendix gives the answer and the tolerance but no method.

The full worked implementations are on the solutions branch, and they are public. They are covered by the same continuous integration as main, so a solution that stops passing shows as a red badge rather than arriving as an email six months later.

They are on a branch rather than on main for one reason: a student who has just read a test name and searched for it should land on the test, not on the answer. Nothing is being withheld.

Anything else

Email avishek.nag@ucd.ie from your institutional address with the course code and institution. Requests are answered in a week or so during term, sooner outside it. If you are assembling slides or a problem set and want variants of the [A] exercises on a different reference model, ask: there is no prepared bank, but the arithmetic modules generate them and I would rather send you working ones than have you re-derive them.

Adopting the book

Appendix D carries a dependency graph and four reading routes through the sixteen chapters, including the six sections to read if you have one afternoon. For a one-semester course the usual cut is Parts I and II in full, Chapter 10 and Chapter 11 from Part III, and one chapter of Part IV chosen to match the course. If you tell me the shape of your module I will tell you what I would cut, which is a faster conversation than reading the appendix.
